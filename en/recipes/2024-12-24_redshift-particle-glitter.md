# Festive glitter from spinning particles (Redshift custom-object instances: flat platonic flakes)

- Source: Top Tip Tuesday — Particle Glitter — https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ouo1xie_Spk (2024-12-24)
- Host / generation: cached NeXus particle reveal (see 2024-12-17) + Redshift Object tag instancing
- Goal: glinting gold/silver dust and flakes.

## Base
Two identical cached emitters: "small" 500 000 particles (r 1 ± 0.5) and "large" 20 000. **Extended Data ▸ Use Rotation, Random**, **Simple Spin** small amounts + variation — the spin is what makes the glints flicker.

## Steps
1. Two long thin **Redshift Area lights** (slight green / red tint) + Redshift camera with DOF. [▶ 1:47](https://youtu.be/ouo1xie_Spk?t=107)
2. Flake geometry: **Platonic** r 3 cm → make editable → squash flat in Y; make 3 copies. [▶ 2:36](https://youtu.be/ouo1xie_Spk?t=156)
3. Both emitters ▸ **Redshift Object tag ▸ Particles ▸ Custom Objects**, Distribution **Random**. Scale ~0.2 (large) and ~0.05 (small). [▶ 3:04](https://youtu.be/ouo1xie_Spk?t=184)
4. Materials: **silver** (white, metalness 1, very low roughness); **gold** (same, orange); **diffuse** (high roughness, very dark color — spreads the highlight, adds depth). [▶ 6:20](https://youtu.be/ouo1xie_Spk?t=380)
5. Large tag: silver + gold + diffuse; small tag: silver + diffuse. [▶ 6:17](https://youtu.be/ouo1xie_Spk?t=377)
6. Judge the effect in motion (stills don't glitter); light color shapes the bokeh. [▶ 7:48](https://youtu.be/ouo1xie_Spk?t=468)
